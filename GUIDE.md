# Jarvis — how to actually use it

> The same content is in the app: **Settings → How to use this app**. This file is
> the version you can read on a laptop.

Capture everything so your head is free, review it on a schedule so nothing
rots, and grade your own decisions so you stop repeating the expensive ones.
Growth is what falls out of doing that consistently — not what you get from a
burst of effort.

Everything is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent to any
server, which is also why the monthly backup below is not optional.

---

## The three loops

### ⚡ Daily — 2 minutes

| When | Do this | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Name the **one** Big Rock. Not five things. One. | Home → Big Rock card (tap to edit) |
| All day | Capture anything that enters your head, in plain language | ⊕ button (bottom right, any screen) |
| Evening | Tap the habits you actually did | Home → 20-Second Log |

The 20-second log writes a real entry for today, so your streak survives a busy
day. That is the point of it — a streak survived beats a perfect day you didn't
have time for.

**Payoff:** two minutes a day buys a searchable record of your own life.

### 🧭 Weekly — 10 minutes, Sunday

Home → *Run a Weekly Review*. Six steps, in order:

1. **Your week in numbers** — days logged, big rocks moved, deep work vs last
   week, sleep, family time, learning, and the wins you logged. Read this before
   you form an opinion about the week. Feelings lie about weeks; numbers don't.
2. **Triage the inbox** — everything you captured becomes → Today, → Planned, or
   deleted. An inbox you never triage turns into a guilt pile.
3. **Clear stale reminders** — anything that came due and no longer matters.
4. **Debts & spends** — chase it, settle it, or write it off. Deliberately.
5. **Reflect** — one line on what you want to change. Saves to your Journal, so
   in six months you can read what you kept promising yourself.
6. **Done.**

**Payoff:** this is the difference between logging your life and steering it.

### 🔐 Monthly — 3 minutes

1. **Settings → Export backup (encrypted).** Pick a passphrase you won't forget.
   Save the file somewhere that is *not* this phone.
2. **Check the badge.** Settings shows days since your last backup; Home nags you
   after 21 days. A cleared browser cache is a deleted life.
3. **Progress → Charts.** Look at one metric over 8 weeks. Trends are real; days
   are noise.

**Payoff:** insurance against the one mistake you cannot undo.

## What each thing is for

### 🔍 Search — magnifier on Home
One box across **everything**: backlog, all 16 tools, journal, goals, people,
checklists, errand runs, portfolio, and every Big Rock and daily win you ever
logged.

*How to get value:* before you ask anyone a question, search your own brain
first. Try a person's name, a topic like `insurance`, or a lesson you
half-remember. If it isn't findable, it isn't a second brain.

### 📅 What's Coming — calendar icon on Home
Every dated thing on one timeline: Overdue / Today / Tomorrow / This week /
Later. Sources: item reminders, goal target dates, decision review dates,
dated favours owed.

*How to get value:* check it once each morning. The badge turns red when
something is overdue. This is the screen that stops surprises.

### ⊕ Quick Capture
Type how you think. It extracts:

- **when** — `tomorrow 6pm`, `friday`, `in 3 days`, `on 5th`, `tonight`, `at 7`, `in 2 hours`
- **how much** — `₹450`, `rs 1200`, `2k`, or a bare number in a money context
- **where** — the destination, from the words you used

Examples:

| You type | It becomes |
|---|---|
| `call Ravi tomorrow 6pm` | 📞 Follow-up · reminder tomorrow 6:00 PM |
| `₹450 dinner` | 💰 Money · ₹450 |
| `buy milk and eggs` | 🛒 Shopping → Grocery |
| `i owe ravi 2k for the trip` | 📤 I Owe · ₹2,000 |
| `kiran owes me 15000` | 📥 Owed to Me · ₹15,000 |
| `car service in 2 weeks` | 🔧 Maintenance → Vehicle · reminder in 14 days |
| `should i switch jobs or stay` | 🧠 Decision |
| `book flight to delhi friday` | 🧳 Travel · reminder Friday |
| `renew passport next month` | 🧹 Life Admin · reminder next month |

Every guess shows as a **tappable chip** — wrong bucket or wrong time is one tap
to fix, and it defaults to 📥 Inbox when it isn't sure.

*How to get value:* never leave the app open to "deal with it later". Capture in
four seconds, sort on Sunday.

### 🎯 Big Rock
One thing per day, editable straight from Home, with a Done toggle.

*How to get value:* set it before you open anything else. *Eat That Frog* — the
hardest, highest-impact task first, while you still have willpower.

### 🧠 Decision Journal — Tools → Decisions
Record the call, the options you weighed, **what you expect to happen**, and a
review date (1 week / 1 month / 3 months / 1 year, or pick one). On that date it
appears in your Command Center and asks what actually happened. You grade it:
better than expected / about right / mixed / I regret it.

*How to get value:* this is the regret engine. Memory rewrites every decision to
look inevitable — this doesn't. After ten reviewed decisions you get a real
regret rate and can see what the bad calls have in common (rushed? alone? under
pressure?).

### 📥 Backlog → Today
**Backlog** is the unsorted inbox. **Today** is what you committed to, in six
sections (Must Do, Quick Wins, Outside, Work, Home, Evening).

*How to get value:* keep Must Do at 3–5 items. A Today list of twenty is a
Backlog wearing a costume.

### 🚗 Errand Run
A trip as a sequence of stops, with items at each one.

*How to get value:* build it before you leave the house. Second trips are the tax
on vague lists.

### 🤝 People
A ledger per person: kind things, hurtful things, and favours owed in either
direction.

*How to get value:* relationships decay silently. Log the favour the day it
happens, then settle it. Not score-keeping — remembering to be fair.

### 🧾 Debts & Spends
Money you owe, money owed to you, one-off spends worth reflecting on.

*How to get value:* log it the moment it happens, while it's still a number and
not yet an awkward conversation.

### 📖 Journal — Daily → Journal
Experiences with what happened, the lesson, tags, and an impact rating. Mistakes
get their own category.

*How to get value:* **log every mistake that cost you money, time or a
relationship — then search it before repeating it.** This is the highest-return
feature in the app. Most people pay the same tuition twice.

### 🏆 Levels, streaks, badges
XP from logged habits, nine ranks, per-habit streaks, 21 badges.

*How to get value:* don't chase XP. It exists to carry you through the first 30
days, until the data itself becomes the reason you log.

### 📊 Charts & Brain Pulse
Eight-week trends per metric, correlations, and short written insights on Home.

*How to get value:* come with a question, not for a browse. "Does sleep actually
change my mood?" — it correlates it and tells you.

## Seven rules that keep this working

1. **Capture beats organise.** A messy inbox you actually write to beats a
   perfect system you avoid.
2. **One Big Rock, not a to-do list.** Days are won by one hard thing, not twelve
   easy ones.
3. **Log the mistake, or pay for it twice.** The Journal compounds faster than
   money.
4. **Numbers before feelings.** Start every review with the data.
5. **Review your decisions, not just your tasks.** Tasks tell you if you were
   busy. Decisions tell you if you were right.
6. **Back up monthly.** Everything lives in one browser on one device.
7. **A streak survived beats a perfect day.** Six taps on Home counts.

---

## If you only do two things

Capture into ⊕ the moment something enters your head, and run the Weekly Review
on Sunday. Those two habits alone make the other fourteen screens worth having.

---

## Known limits

- **Reminders only fire while the app is open.** There is no push server and no
  background scheduler, so a reminder set for 3am fires the next time you open
  the app. *What's Coming* exists precisely because of this — it is the reliable
  way to see what's due. Enable notifications in Settings to get an OS
  notification for anything that came due while the app was open.
- **Data is per-browser, per-device.** No sync. Moving phones means export →
  import.
- **Search is substring-based**, not fuzzy. `insurance` finds "insurance"; it
  won't find "insurence".
